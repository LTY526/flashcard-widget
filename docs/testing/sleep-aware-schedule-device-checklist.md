# Sleep-aware schedule manual device checklist

Run on an iPhone with a configured rectangular Lock Screen widget.

- [ ] Suspend the app past at least two dates, reopen it, and confirm Deck
      Detail immediately matches the widget card.
- [ ] Confirm a card spanning sleep remains visible until its shifted wake-side
      due date.
- [ ] Tap Next during sleep; confirm the card changes immediately and its
      successor is one full awake interval after wake.
- [ ] Confirm the widget always has three rows and uses an em dash for a missing
      secondary or tertiary value.
- [ ] Confirm long quaternary text is fully visible in Deck Detail and absent
      from the widget.
- [ ] Confirm Upcoming shows Current and every future row; Past excludes Current,
      is newest-first, and loads ten more rows per tap.
